# New customer onboarding (course)

1. Welcome email goes out automatically from Kit within 5 minutes of purchase.
2. Day 2: "how to get the most from the course" email.
3. Day 7: check-in email asking one question: what's your biggest email problem?
4. Day 14: invitation to join Growth Club at the member price.

If a customer hasn't logged in by day 7, Sam sends a personal note.
