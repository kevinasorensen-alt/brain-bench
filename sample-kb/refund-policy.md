# Refund policy

- Courses and template packs: full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked.
- After 14 days: no refunds, but the customer can swap the template pack for a different one once.
- Growth Club: no refunds for past months; cancelling stops the next charge.
- Refunds over $100 need Ava's approval. Sam can approve anything up to $100.
- Every refund is logged in the Refunds sheet the same day.
