# Tools we use

- Email platform: Kit (formerly ConvertKit), for the newsletter and course emails.
- Payments and checkout: Lemon Squeezy.
- Course hosting: a members area on the website, built on WordPress with a membership plugin.
- Notes and SOPs: an Obsidian vault shared through a synced folder.
- Scheduling social posts: Buffer, free plan, LinkedIn and Instagram only.

We do not use paid ads. All growth is organic: newsletter, LinkedIn and partnerships.
