# About Northwind Studio

Northwind Studio is a two-person online business that sells marketing courses and templates to small-business owners. It was founded in 2024 by Ava Lin (founder, teaches the courses) and Sam Ortiz (operations and support).

Main products: the Email Basics course, the Content Calendar template pack and the monthly Growth Club membership.

The studio is fully remote. The team works Monday to Thursday; Friday is reserved for writing and planning, with no customer calls.
